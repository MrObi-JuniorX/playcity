How to play

  • Flick. On your turn pull back from one of your lit caps and let go. A longer pull is a harder flick.
  • Hit the ball first. Barging into an opponent before the ball is a foul: free kick, or a penalty inside your own box.
  • The ball is a button. It glides flat on the table and never lifts, because it is lower than the edge of the caps. Spin makes it bend a little as it slides, like a curling stone.
  • The board. The wooden sides are solid: caps and the button bounce off them.
  • Keepers are live. Slide and dive while the ball is moving. Diving has a short cooldown. Against the computer, both WASD and the arrow keys move your keeper.
  • Match clock. Set the half length on the setup screen, or choose Off to play without halves. Open Menu (or press Esc) to pause, end the match or quit to team selection.
  • Kick-offs. Before every kick-off the defending side can drag its caps anywhere in its own half, outside its penalty area and the centre circle, then press Ready. The computer sets up instantly. The match clock waits while the caps are arranged.
  • Goal kicks. Before a goal kick the other side starts from its formation and can drag its caps anywhere outside the kicker’s penalty area, then press Ready, just like at a kick-off.
  • Set pieces. Every throw-in, corner, goal kick, free kick or penalty you take starts with the taker picked up: drag the lit cap (or tap inside the yellow area) to move it, then tap Ready on the card beside it (or Enter) and flick. Throw-in and corner takers stay behind the lines. At a kick-off, Move taker (or M) moves the taker.
  • The referee. Every decision gets a whistle and a signal. A second foul by the same player is a yellow card, a fourth is a red card and the player is sent off.
  • Restarts. Throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, free kicks and penalties are taken by the highlighted player only.
  • Camera. Tap 2D for a flat top view and 3D to go back to the angled view. Open View to tilt the table down to pitch level, turn it, zoom, or pick a preset such as Low 3D. You can also drag empty table to orbit, pinch to zoom, or use Q/E and R/F.
  • Your keeper. Drag your goalkeeper anywhere inside the six-yard box. The keys and pad still slide it across goal and dive.
