FloodlightFC

Eleven-a-side night football. Pick a manager, a shape and a playstyle, then play it with your thumbs.

Touch controls
Attacking
Left thumb
Floating stick, drop it anywhere. Double-tap that side to stop dead and face goal
Shoot
Hold for power, let go in the green band. Slide off the button before letting go: up chip, down finesse, right Stunning shot, left fake shot
Pass
Aimed with the stick. Slide right for a Stunning pass, left for a one-two (then Pass again to find the runner). Pass during a shot wind-up is a fake
Through
Into space. Slide up to loft it over the line, right for a Stunning through ball
Loft
Over the top, or a cross. Slide right for a driven Stunning cross
Skill
Tap for a body feint, swipe for your four moves, hold to shield the ball
Sprint
Hold to run. Double-tap to knock the ball on
Press early
Pass or Shoot while the ball is on its way plays it first time
Defending
Tackle
Tap for a standing tackle, double-tap to slide. From behind it is a foul
Press
Hold to close down. Slide up to call the whole team to press
2nd man
Hold to send a teammate. Slide up to bring the keeper out
Jockey
Hold to stay on your feet against skills
Switch
Change to the player nearest the ball
Left thumb
Double-tap for a shoulder charge
Top left
− +
Attack and defence level
A / B
Switch sub-tactic
Trap
Offside trap: the back line steps up
Set pieces
Stick
Aim. Shoot for goal on free kicks and penalties, Loft to cross, Pass to play it short
